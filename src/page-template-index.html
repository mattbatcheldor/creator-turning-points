About this archive

Creator Turning Points is the third archive, alongside Brand Turning Points and Partnership Turning Points. It puts the creator at the centre: what they made, what became possible and which part of the outcome can be traced.

Seven editions contain 100 moments from @@FIRST_YEAR@@ to @@LAST_YEAR@@. The evidence ranges from original uploads and public rule changes to chart positions, donations and reported sales. Every case separates its media record from its outcome source, and gives the headline number a definition.

Creators rarely work alone. Performers, editors, developers, moderators, production teams and commercial partners help make the work possible. The two contributions in each case name part of that collaboration, rather than an exhaustive production credit.

This is a selection, not a ranking or a representative sample. The rating describes what the public record supports. It does not rate a creator’s talent or a programme’s worth. With no modelled effects or representative surveys in this selection, the lessons are questions to test in a future programme.
