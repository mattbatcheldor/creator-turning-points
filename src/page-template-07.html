Why this edition exists

Ninety cases in, most of this archive happened in Britain and the United States. Edition 07 closes the series with ten that didn’t. They come from nine countries on four continents, and several were made with a phone and no budget at all. Many of the formats the West later adopted, live tipping, virtual creators and shopping inside a stream, were working at scale here first.

01 / The finding

The edge invented formats the centre later copied.

Six of these ten cases rest on reach: 14.1 million subscribers, Africa’s first comedy channel to a million, Southeast Asia’s first ten million and TikTok’s most followed person. Two get close to money: $9,000 a month from viewers’ gifts in 2014, and $1.7 billion sold in twelve hours in 2021. Two are archival.

Three of the formats here travelled to the West: live gifts on a Korean eating stream, virtual creators from Japan and live shopping from China. In each case the idea was working at scale years before Western platforms built the features to support it.

Five of the ten were made with almost no budget: a phone in Nairobi, a webcam in Seoul, a cousin and a camera in Port Harcourt, a factory worker’s shrug in Turin and a bedroom in Piauí. What they had was a format that needed no translation.

04 / The evidence

What a turning point at the edge can and can’t prove

Moment, milestone, result

A first upload or a record is a moment. A milestone, like a subscriber count, shows attention. Only a result shows what changed for the creator, the platform or a brand. Each case says which of the three the record supports.

Whose number it is

Many figures come from the platforms, the creators themselves or the press. The source for every outcome is named, so you can judge it. Estimates of creator income are marked as estimates.

Attention is not income

Views, subscribers and concurrent viewers count attention. Paid subscriptions, sales and chart positions get closer to money. The rating says which kind of number a case rests on.

The evidence rating

Every case gets one of five ratings, strongest first: modelled effect, sales trend, survey, reach only and archival. A date or a deal term is marked as a legacy fact so it isn’t read as a result.

The creator comes first

Each case puts the creator at the centre: what they made, what changed because of it, and who else was involved. Brand partnerships appear when the creator drove them.

How cases earn a place

Each case changed what was possible for creators, platforms or brands, and left a public record. Creator stories already told in Brand Turning Points or Partnership Turning Points are not repeated here; The Index links to them.

05 / What lasts

A hundred turning points, three lessons.

Across seven editions, the same three things recur. First, creators find the use for a format before the platform does, from Twitch Plays Pokémon to Khaby Lame. Second, attention is easy to count and hard to turn into proof: only a handful of the hundred cases show a result anyone measured properly. Third, the creators who last own something, an audience they can reach directly, a product or a company, so a platform’s decision can’t switch them off.
